Help

Entering an invoice

Use New Invoice. Amounts must be plain numbers such as 118000.00 (no commas or symbols). Dates use YYYY-MM-DD.

Vendors

Only active vendors can be selected. See all vendors, including inactive ones, on the Vendors page. A vendor that is not listed must be requested before an invoice can be entered.

Invoices

Saved invoices start as Pending Approval and cannot be edited or deleted from this screen.
